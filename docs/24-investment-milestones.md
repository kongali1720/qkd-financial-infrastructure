# KFIP Investment Milestones and Funding Controls

**Planning period:** 24 months  
**Indicative program budget:** IDR 15 billion

## Funding Allocation

| Stage | Indicative amount | Primary gate |
|---|---:|---|
| Foundation and validation | IDR 2.00 billion | Legal, market, and architecture review |
| MVP development | IDR 4.00 billion | Technical acceptance |
| Integration and pilot | IDR 4.00 billion | Verified provider integration and pilot |
| Commercial readiness | IDR 2.70 billion | Customer and operational readiness |
| Contingency reserve | IDR 2.30 billion | Separate approval |
| **Total** | **IDR 15.00 billion** | |

The contingency reserve is not an automatic final-stage expenditure.
It remains controlled and may be used only after documented approval.

## Stage 1 — Months 1–3

Deliverables:
- Corporate and regulatory review.
- Customer discovery.
- Product requirements.
- Architecture baseline.
- Core team recruitment.
- Development environment.

Acceptance evidence:
- Approved requirements.
- Architecture review.
- Legal assessment.
- Identified pilot prospects.

## Stage 2 — Months 4–8

Deliverables:
- Payment orchestration API.
- Double-entry ledger.
- Reconciliation engine.
- Corporate dashboard.
- Identity and access controls.
- Audit logging.

Acceptance evidence:
- Automated test reports.
- Balanced journal tests.
- Idempotency tests.
- Security test results.
- Demonstrable end-to-end sandbox workflow.

## Stage 3 — Months 9–14

Deliverables:
- Official provider sandbox integration.
- Webhook handling.
- Reconciliation validation.
- Independent security assessment.
- Controlled customer pilot.

Acceptance evidence:
- Partner authorization.
- Integration test report.
- Pilot report.
- Security remediation evidence.

## Stage 4 — Months 15–20

Deliverables:
- SLA and support processes.
- Disaster recovery tests.
- Commercial contracts.
- Customer onboarding.
- Cost and revenue analysis.

Acceptance evidence:
- Signed customer agreements where obtained.
- Service performance reports.
- Operational readiness assessment.
- Updated financial forecast.

## Stage 5 — Months 21–24

Deliverables:
- Platform capacity improvements.
- Additional provider integrations.
- PQC proof of concept.
- QKD laboratory feasibility report.
- Expansion plan.

Acceptance evidence:
- Research report.
- Independent technical review.
- Customer retention and revenue metrics.
- Expansion investment recommendation.

## Funding Control Principles

1. Each stage requires an approved budget.
2. Evidence must be reviewed before the next stage.
3. Unused funds remain with the company.
4. Material budget changes require documented approval.
5. No milestone guarantees regulatory approval or commercial revenue.
6. Payment and share issuance arrangements must comply with corporate law.
