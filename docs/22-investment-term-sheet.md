## 2. Investment Purpose

Funding is intended to develop and commercialize the Kongali Financial
Infrastructure Platform (KFIP), including:

- Corporate payment orchestration software.
- Double-entry ledger.
- Reconciliation engine.
- Banking and payment provider API integrations.
- Cloud and cybersecurity infrastructure.
- Enterprise pilot programs.
- Legal and regulatory readiness.
- PQC and QKD research.

## 3. Proposed Amount

IDR 15 billion is an indicative planning scenario.
The final amount is subject to investor approval, due diligence,
definitive agreements, and corporate approvals.

## 7. Funding Controls

The proposed funding stages are:

- Stage 1: IDR 2.00 billion — foundation and validation.
- Stage 2: IDR 4.00 billion — MVP development.
- Stage 3: IDR 4.00 billion — integration and controlled pilot.
- Stage 4: IDR 2.70 billion — commercial readiness.
- Contingency reserve: IDR 2.30 billion — separately controlled.

The parties must determine a legally valid payment and share issuance
mechanism. Staged funding must not be implemented in a manner inconsistent
with Indonesian company law or share subscription requirements.
