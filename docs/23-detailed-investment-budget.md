## I. Contingency Reserve — IDR 2.30 billion

The reserve shall remain separately controlled.
Release requires documented justification and approval under the
company's agreed governance process.

## Budget Governance

- Monthly actual-versus-budget reporting.
- Purchase orders and supporting invoices.
- Approval thresholds for material expenditure.
- Related-party transaction disclosures.
- Quarterly investor reporting.
- Reforecast when material assumptions change.

The budget must be validated against vendor quotations, hiring plans,
tax treatment, and actual implementation requirements before investment closing.
