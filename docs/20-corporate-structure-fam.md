# Corporate Structure and Governance
## KFIP — PT FAM TIMUR INDONESIA

**Status:** DRAFT — SUBJECT TO LEGAL AND CORPORATE DUE DILIGENCE  
**Investment scenario:** IDR 15 billion  
**Program period:** 24 months

## 1. Proposed Structure

PT FAM TIMUR INDONESIA is the proposed investment-receiving entity.
KFIP is initially planned as a separately managed and financially tracked
business unit within the company.

The proposed governance structure is:

1. General Meeting of Shareholders (RUPS).
2. Board of Commissioners.
3. Board of Directors.
4. KFIP Business Unit.
5. Technology, Finance, Security, Compliance, and Commercial functions.

The final structure must be reconciled with the company's current articles
of association, shareholder register, corporate approvals, and applicable law.

## 2. Ring-fencing Controls

KFIP shall maintain:

- A dedicated project cost center.
- Project-level accounting and cash-flow reports.
- A separately approved project budget.
- An inventory of software, intellectual property, and licenses.
- Written procurement and expenditure approvals.
- Related-party transaction disclosures.
- Monthly management reporting.
- Quarterly investor reporting.

Ring-fencing is an internal governance and accounting control. It does not
create a separate legal entity or isolate KFIP liabilities from the company.

## 3. Management Functions

| Function | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| CEO / Directors | Corporate strategy and accountability |
| CTO | Product architecture and engineering |
| Finance Controller | Budget, cash flow, and reporting |
| Security Lead | Cybersecurity and technical risk |
| Compliance Lead | Regulatory assessment and controls |
| Business Development | Customers and partnerships |
| KFIP Project Lead | Delivery and milestone management |

Some functions may initially be provided by qualified contractors or advisors.

## 4. Investor Governance

Investor rights must be documented in the share subscription agreement,
shareholders' agreement, and applicable corporate documents.

Potential rights for negotiation:

- Shareholder voting rights.
- Dividend rights under applicable law.
- Periodic financial and operational reporting.
- Reserved matters and material expenditure controls.
- Pre-emptive or participation rights where applicable.
- Negotiated share transfer protections.

Investor consent rights must be implemented through lawful corporate
procedures and must not conflict with mandatory Indonesian law.

## 5. Important Ownership Distinction

An investor subscribing for shares in PT FAM TIMUR INDONESIA acquires
rights in that company as a whole, not merely in the KFIP business unit.

If an investor requires exclusive ownership exposure to KFIP, the parties
should evaluate a dedicated subsidiary or another legally reviewed structure.
