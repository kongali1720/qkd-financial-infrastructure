# Chapter 1 - Introduction

## 1.1 Background

Financial institutions rely on cryptographic mechanisms to protect transaction confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity.

Advances in quantum computing motivate the transition toward quantum-resistant security architectures.

## 1.2 Problem Statement

Existing public-key infrastructure may face future quantum threats. Financial organizations need practical migration strategies that preserve interoperability and operational security.

## 1.3 Research Objectives

1. Examine the foundations of Quantum Key Distribution.
2. Evaluate its suitability for selected financial communication links.
3. Investigate standardized post-quantum cryptography.
4. Propose a hybrid QKD-PQC financial security architecture.
5. Identify implementation constraints and research gaps.

## 1.4 Scope

This study addresses secure communication and cryptographic key management. It does not claim to implement a production banking or quantum network.
